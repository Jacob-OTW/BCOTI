# Collection of Mounting Clips
The BCOTI is meant to be very modular in regard to what devices it can be mounted on, this folder contains some ready-to-go designs.
I've you have a device or lens type that is not support yet, feel free to reach out to me.

## List of Clips

### PVS14_InfinityRing_Clip
- Uses an M2 screw to clamp on the infinity-ring (also called a D-Lock Ring).
- Requires 1x 3.6mmOD M2x3 insert (same kind as for the body, found in the BOM), and an M2 screw with a length of 12mm or longer. The Side with the small X is the side you put the insert into.

### Baldur_InfinityRing_Clip
- Same as `PVS14_InfinityRing_Clip` only with a slightly larger ID of 38mm and 8mm length to support Baldur Housing.

### Old_Fenrir_InfinityRing_Clip
- Same as `PVS14_InfinityRing_Clip` only with a slightly larger ID of 40mm and 8mm length to support older version of the Fenrir Mono housing.

### ANVIS_Front_Lens_Clamp
- Confirmed to work with Anvis 6/9/9(4lobed) lenses.
- Requires 1x 3.6mmOD M2x3 insert (same kind as for the body, found in the BOM), and an M2 screw with a length of 12mm or longer. The Side with the small X is the side you put the insert into.

### AGM_PVS7_InfinityRing_Clip.step
- Uses an M2 screw to clamp onto the infinity-ring (also called a D-Lock Ring) of an AGM PVS7 Bioc.
- Requires 1x 3.6mmOD M2x3 insert (same kind as for the body, found in the BOM), and an M2 screw with a length of 12mm or longer. The Side with the small X is the side you put the insert into.


### PVS14_FrontLens_Clip.step
- Uses an M2 screw to clamp on the objective lens itself, this isn't ideal, as mounting the BCOTI can also mess with the focus of your lens. 
This does allow for pretty much any housing using standard (non-RPO style) PVS14 glass to use the BCOTI, at the cost of a non-ideal mounting position.
- Requires 1x 3.6mmOD M2x3 insert (same kind as for the body, found in the BOM), and an M2 screw with a length of 12mm or longer. The Side with the small X is the side you put the insert into.

### PVS1531_Shell_Clip.step
- Uses an M2 screw to clamp onto the pod itself.
- Requires 1x 3.6mmOD M2x3 insert (same kind as for the body, found in the BOM), and an M2 screw with a length of 12mm or longer. The Side with the small X is the side you put the insert into.

###PVS14_InfinityRing_Clip_for_Infinity_IRIS
- Uses an M2 screw to clamp onto the pod itself.
- Same as `PVS14_InfinityRing_Clip` but is enlongated to accomodate the Infinity Cap iris
